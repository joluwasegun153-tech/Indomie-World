Indomie Special Recipe

Classic Indomie and Fried Egg

Enjoy a delicious plate of classic Indomie noodles served with a tasty fried egg. This simple recipe is perfect for breakfast, lunch or dinner.

Preparation Time

15 Minutes

Difficulty

Easy

Ingredients

  • ✓ 1 pack of Indomie noodles
  • ✓ 1 fresh egg
  • ✓ 1 small onion
  • ✓ Fresh pepper (optional)
  • ✓ Vegetable oil
  • ✓ Indomie seasoning

Preparation Steps

01. Boil water in a pot.

02. Add the noodles and cook according to the package instructions.

03. Add the seasoning and stir gently.

04. Prepare the egg separately until fully cooked.

05. Serve the noodles with the cooked egg.

Kitchen Guide

Cooking Tips

Simple tips to make your Indomie meal more enjoyable.

🍜

Perfect Noodles

Follow the cooking time on the package to get noodles with a pleasant texture.

🥚

Add Fresh Ingredients

Try adding vegetables and a fully cooked egg to make your meal more interesting.

✨

Serve Fresh

Enjoy your noodles shortly after preparation while they are warm.
