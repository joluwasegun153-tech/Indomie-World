How to Prepare Your Favourite Indomie

Cooking Indomie is quick and easy. Follow these simple steps to enjoy a delicious meal in minutes.

Boil Ther Water

Pour clean water into a pot and bring it to a boil.

Add the Noodles

Put the Indomie noodles into the boiling water and cook until soft.

Add the Seasoning

Add the seasoning according to the packet instructions and stir well.

Serve and Enjoy

Serve your delicious noodles and add your favourite toppings.
