Learn the Basics of Cooking Indomie

Preparing Indomie is simple and enjoyable. With the right ingredients and cooking techniques, you can create a delicious meal in just a few minutes.

Step 1:Prepare Your Ingredients

Before cooking, gather your noodles, seasoning, water and any extra ingredients you want to add.

Step 2:Cook Your Noodles

Follow the instructions on the noodle package. Avoid overcooking so your noodles maintain a pleasant texture.

Step 3:Add Your Favourite Ingredients

Make your meal more interesting with vegetables, eggs, chicken or other ingredients.

Step-by-Step Cooking Guide

How to Prepare Classic Indomi

EASY 15 MINUTES

Classic Indomie & Fried Egg

Learn how to prepare delicious classic Indomie served with a tasty fried egg.

Classic Indomie & Fried Egg

Ingredients

  • 1 pack of Indomie noodles
  • 1 fresh egg
  • 1 small carrot
  • Spring onions
  • Indomie seasoning
  • Water

Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Boil water in a cooking pot.

Step 2: Add the Indomie noodles and cook until soft, following the packet instructions.

Step 3: Add the seasoning and mix properly.

Step 4: Prepare your egg separately with the help of an adult.

Step 5: Serve your noodles with the egg and vegetables.

Your delicious Indomie meal is ready!

Cooking Tips

Secrets to Making Delicious Indomie

Don't Overcook:

Follow the recommended cooking time on the package.

Mix Your Seasoning Well:

Stir gently to distribute the seasoning.

Use the Right Amount of Water:

Follow the package instructions for the desired texture.

Use Fresh Vegetables:

Add carrots, cabbage, green peppers or other vegetables.

Try Different Ingredients:

Experiment with different ingredient combinations.

Serve Fresh:

Enjoy your noodles shortly after preparation.
